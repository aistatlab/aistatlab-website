01 / WHO WE ARE

Rigorous thinking.
Meaningful impact.

At the Artificial Intelligence & Statistics Lab, we connect the mathematical rigor of statistics with the capabilities of modern artificial intelligence. Our research spans fundamental methods and applications in healthcare, vision, audio, and intelligent industrial systems.
